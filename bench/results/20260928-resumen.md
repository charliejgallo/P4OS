# First day with the board (2026-09-28)

## The board

| | |
|---|---|
| Chip | ESP32-P4 **revision v1.3** (not v3.x): P4OS and p4bench build with the `rev1_3` profile |
| CPU | 360 MHz |
| Flash | 32 MB, GigaDevice (0xc84019) |
| PSRAM | 32 MB, AP, 200 MHz |
| Factory firmware | IDF 5.5.2 with ESP-Brookesia. Backup in `bench/results/raw/factory-full.bin`, not in the repo; SHA-256 d8e7288c…a705, checked against the chip's MD5 |
| C6 | Answers over 4-bit SDIO at 40 MHz. It offers Wi-Fi **and BLE** (HCI over SDIO) |

## Measured

**Memory**
- PSRAM: memcpy 67 MB/s, memset 146 MB/s, read 165 MB/s.
- Free at boot: 276 KB of internal RAM, 26,9 MB of PSRAM.

**Chip temperature:** 31,4 °C at rest.

**Screen**
- 55 Hz measured refresh.
- Filling the whole screen: CPU 14,6 ms, PPA 5,2 ms, DMA2D 4,4 ms.

**Touch**
- The GT911 answers at 0x14 at boot. After a reset through GPIO23 without driving INT, it comes back at **0x5D**.
- The board's support code probes both addresses at start, so P4OS is fine. But nothing may reset the GT911 at runtime without initialising it again: the driver floods the console with errors, and at 115200 baud that blocks it.

**I2C (board bus):** ES8311 at 0x18, ES7210 at 0x40, GT911.

**microSD** (16 GB, FAT32, 32 KB clusters, 4-bit, 40 MHz)

| How it reads | Speed |
|---|---|
| Raw sectors | 16–17 MB/s |
| `read()` | 16 MB/s |
| `fread()`, newlib's 128 B buffer | 1,6 MB/s |
| `fread()`, 8 KB buffer (`CONFIG_FATFS_VFS_FSTAT_BLKSIZE=8192`, now in P4OS) | 10,8 MB/s |
| `fread()`, unbuffered | 0,08 MB/s |

Writes run at 4–9 MB/s.

**JPEG engine (720×1280):** encode 19 ms, decode 10,5 ms.

**PPA**
- ×3 scaling from 184×224: 4,25 ms.
- Rotating a whole screen: 41 ms.

**Audio**
- 1 kHz tone works.
- Full duplex works: speaker to microphones 28 dB above the quiet level.

**Battery ADC:** reads 4,2 V with **no battery**. That is the charger's output: P4OS must not decide there is a battery from the voltage.

## LVGL (benchmark average, lvB variant: FreeRTOS with 2 draw units)

| Setup | fps | render ms | flush ms | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| portrait, PPA copy, 2 FB, 80 rows | 15 | 27 | 68 | good |
| portrait, CPU copy, 2 FB, 80 rows | 17 | 33 | 42 | good |
| landscape, PPA rotate+copy, 2 FB | 13 | 29 | 69 | good |
| landscape, CPU rotate+copy, 2 FB | 12 | 31 | 77 | good |
| portrait, DMA2D (my own `esp_async_fbcpy`) into the back buffer, 2 FB | 20 | 27 | 30 | **garbage** (photos): it doesn't work as written |
| **portrait, DMA2D (esp_lcd `draw_bitmap`, async, done callback), 1 FB, 128 rows** | **35** | 28 | 3 | **good, no visible tearing** |
| **landscape, same, with each chunk rotated in internal RAM first** | **22** | 28 | 38 | good |

**Notes**
- **The DMA2D copy is asynchronous.** `draw_bitmap` returns at once, so `lv_display_flush_ready` has to come from `on_color_trans_done`. Before that fix, LVGL reused buffers still being copied: chunks were dropped ("previous draw operation is not finished") and the screen showed rubbish.
- **Tearing:** with one frame buffer, only the raw test (a white bar sweeping) shows it. The LVGL benchmark looked clean to the eye.
- **Landscape:** a chunk of logical rows lands on the panel as a tall column of 1280 short lines, and PSRAM is slow with short writes. That, not the rotation itself, is where the 38 ms go.
- **Text:** "Screen sized text" renders at 4 fps (86 ms), and the scrolling text looked a little jerky. That is the CPU drawing text at 360 MHz; to tune later (glyph cache, text drawn once and moved as an image).

**Decision for P4OS**
- DMA2D through `esp_lcd_panel_draw_bitmap`, async with the done callback, one frame buffer, 128-row draw buffers.
- In landscape, each chunk is rotated in internal RAM first.
- `LV_DRAW_LAYER_SIMPLE_BUF_SIZE=92160`, as LVGL asked on screen.

## Network
- **Wi-Fi through the C6:** connects in 4,1 s, RSSI -29/-42 dBm, channel 6, 802.11ax.
- **C6 firmware version:** `hosted fw` gets no answer (RPC timeout). The factory C6 firmware probably doesn't have that call.
- **Test bench bug:** `wifi up` overflowed the console task's 4 KB stack; it has 16 KB now.
- NTP and TLS still to run with the network up.

## Touch, microphone, backlight, sound (hands-on)
- **Touch:** 59 reports/s, up to 4 fingers, x 0–701 and y 1–1274. The right edge (701 of 719) is worth a dedicated edge test.
- **Microphone:** a voice comes in around -18 dB; a clap saturates at 30 dB of gain. P4OS needs lower gain or AGC.
- **Backlight:** the sweep went up and down smoothly (seen).
- **Speaker:** the 100 Hz–8 kHz sweep sounded fine (heard).
